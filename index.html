Fun Stuff

When I’m not working on my computer or building something, you can usually find me doing one of two things: mountain biking or Jiu-Jitsu. So much of how our lives play out is shaped by what we do in our spare time and the lessons we learn along the way. Cycling is my favorite way to clear my head. When I’m working through tough problems—real problems that need to be handled right now—Jiu-Jitsu is where I go to reset, learn, and grow, while also helping others on their path. It’s a place where everyone makes each other better because of their uniqueness. We all take the same class, but we interpret things differently and apply the concepts in our own ways. Programming, cycling, and Jiu-Jitsu all share something in common: consistency equals progression.

Work Stuff

This is what I do—not build decks, but take my ideas and create meaningful projects that fill a need or people enjoy using.
